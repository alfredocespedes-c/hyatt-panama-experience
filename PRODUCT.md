# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React + TypeScript as a static GitHub Pages frontend. Supabase is the delegated backend for response storage because GitHub Pages cannot safely hold database credentials or accept writes by itself.

## Users

Hyatt and PepsiCo event attendees scanning a printed QR code on a mobile phone at Hyatt Regency Panama City. Their job is to enter a short branded experience, understand the venue context, and submit four contact fields quickly.

## Product Purpose

Create a memorable QR-led arrival: a short architectural immersion travels from Panama City to Hyatt Regency Panama City and resolves in the Regency Ballroom, then transitions directly into a four-step form. Success means the experience loads reliably on event Wi-Fi/mobile data, feels premium, and stores each complete response for export.

## Positioning

The video and form are one continuous scene: the projection surface inside the ballroom becomes the interface rather than sending attendees to a generic external form.

## Operating Context

The experience is intended for Monday, October 19, 2026 at 8:00 AM in Panama City. It opens from a printed QR code on mobile browsers. The first prototype is local; the intended deployment is GitHub Pages with a stable public URL.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Short mobile-first immersive opening with a user tap to begin, required for reliable browser audio and fullscreen-like playback.
- Destination venue: Regency Ballroom at Hyatt Regency Panama City, published by Hyatt as 483 m² with capacity up to 400 guests.
- Four one-question-per-screen fields: Name of General Manager; Name of Food & Beverage Director; Hotel Name; e-Mail contact.
- Store only those four responses plus technical submission metadata.
- Publicly available visual references only. Do not ship copyrighted Hyatt photography, Hyatt or PepsiCo logos, Google Earth imagery, or third-party video without permission or a compatible license.
- The exact aerial-to-ballroom sequence must use an original/stylized architectural render unless licensed source footage is supplied.
- GitHub Pages hosts static files only; durable response storage requires Supabase or another external API.

## Brand Commitments

Hyatt and PepsiCo are the named event participants. The chosen tone is restrained architectural elegance. Co-branding must not imply an unauthorized endorsement; use official marks only when the organizer supplies or authorizes them.

## Evidence on Hand

- Public Hyatt meeting-page facts and gallery references.
- Hotel location: Calle 52 and Ricardo Arias, Panama City; OpenStreetMap coordinates approximately 8.9815341, -79.5243317.
- No licensed hotel footage, logos, ballroom images, or brand manual have been supplied.

## Product Principles

- Make the transition itself explain the journey; avoid introductory copy before the experience.
- Preserve continuity from film to form so the survey feels projected inside the ballroom.
- Optimize first for mobile reliability and low bandwidth, then spectacle.
- Ask for one answer at a time and preserve progress.
- Keep data collection minimal, explicit, and exportable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide captions or meaningful text alternatives, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion behavior, adequate contrast, and a skip-video control. Never make the video the only route to the form.
